# Progression & Resources

> [!info] Guideline
> Categorize in-game currencies, acquisition methods, expenditure paths, and character stat progression

- **Primary Currency:** Steps
- **Secondary Currency:** Coins
## Economic Inflow/Outflow Matrix

| Resource/Currency | Sources                                           | Expenditures                  | Cap/Inventory Limit                                             |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Steps             | The player taking steps while the game is running | Recipe unlocks                | Infinite, but the steps have target limits for specific unlocks |
| Coins             | Earned by the player by serving items in the menu | Recipe and decoration unlocks | Infinite, based on how much can be displayed by the UI          |
