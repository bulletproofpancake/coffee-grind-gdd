## Target Audience
### Primary Audience
The primary audience of this game is the casual market. Being an idle/incremental game, there is a low barrier of entry for most people to start playing the game.
### Secondary Audience
The secondary audience for this game are those who are interested in gamified tasks, since the game asks the user to reach a certain amount of steps in order to progress through the game which also serves as their reward.
## Target Platforms
### Mobile (Android)
The game will be released on mobile platforms as it makes use of the device's sensors in order to determine the steps of the player.
# Core Pillars
